# Sources

Everything in this repository was written by us, or is the bundled three.js library. Both ant farms are drawn entirely
by code: there are no models, textures or images, and nothing in them is generated by AI. The pictures in `media/` are
screenshots of the pages themselves.

## Code

- `index.html`, `colony.js` (the 3D colony), `2d/index.html` (the 2D glass farm), `server.py`: ours, MIT (see
  `LICENSE`).
- The ants, soil face, meadow, plants, visitors and sky backdrop of the 3D colony: our own geometry and shader code in
  `colony.js`, MIT. The ants are our own cartoon design.
- `vendor/`: three.js r187 (npm `three@0.187.0`), `build/three.module.js` and `build/three.core.js`, unchanged. MIT,
  © 2010-2026 three.js authors; see `vendor/LICENSE-three.txt`.
